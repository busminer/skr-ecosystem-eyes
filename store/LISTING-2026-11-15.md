## Long description

SKR Eyes shows the SKR staking vault as it actually is, second by second, and where you stand in it. Your wallet tells you what you hold. This tells you what the vault is doing. And since 1.6, what the dApp Store's guardians are doing.

The guardians, live. Every release to the Solana dApp Store is asked for on chain, and every guardian votes on it on chain. SKR Eyes shows it as it happens: each app arrives under its store name and icon, Solana Mobile, Jito and Helius step up to it one after another, and with every vote it turns greener, or red where a guardian says no. Tap an app for its transactions, tap a guardian for its pace, or replay the last hour, six hours or day. Far behind them stands the dApp Store itself, its cube and Solana Mobile's name in the haze. This very build carries its own receipt: every guardian's vote on it and the seconds it took. Every vote shown is the guardian's own, read from the chain; SKR Eyes adds nothing to it.

The living vault. Every stake falls into the vault as a phone with the staker's .skr name under it; every exit hangs above it, cooling for its 48 hours, and drops when it is withdrawn. Larger stakes fall larger and slower. Nothing here is drawn from imagination: every phone is a finalized move on chain, and you can tap any of them for its receipt.

Where you stand. Everybody who stakes, in order: by stake, by what each position has received, and by places moved since yesterday's scan. The treasury-size wallets are set aside, so number one on the list is a person. Your own place rides at the top of it, and the radar puts everyone on one dial: six rings by tier, your phone gold among them.

Live from the vault. The feed of stakes, exits and withdrawals as they finalize, filtered by kind and size, with the day's biggest moves pinned on top. Large moves arrive with a sound and a short buzz on any screen, or switch them off.

Your staker card. Your .skr name, your days in stake, your weight in the vault and where you stand among people, on a card you can share as a picture. Three switches decide what leaves the phone: hide your name, hide your amount, or keep your place off the card.

Quiet check-ins. Up to three a day, at hours you choose, an invitation to look at your own position. Set on the phone itself; no address is sent anywhere for them. Off with one switch.

Reading only. SKR Eyes does not stake, unstake or withdraw, and it builds no transaction of any kind. Connecting a wallet only tells the app which address to show, and you can paste any public address instead.

Eleven languages: English, Deutsch, 中文, Русский, Español, Português, Türkçe, Tiếng Việt, Indonesia, 한국어, 日本語. The app follows your phone's language and can be switched by hand at the end of Me.

Built by one Seeker, on our own indexer, read at finalized commitment. Every number carries its source. SKR Eyes never asks for a seed phrase, never holds keys and never takes custody of funds.

SKR Eyes is an independent product. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Solana Foundation, Solana Mobile, Jito or Helius. Their names and marks, the dApp Store's among them, belong to them and are shown only to identify whose store it is and whose vote is whose.

## New in this version — 1.6.0

```
New tab: Guardians. Every release to the dApp Store is voted on chain by the store's guardians, and SKR Eyes now shows it as it happens: each app under its store name and icon, each guardian's vote turning it green or red at its real second, and a replay of the last hour, six hours or day.

This build carries its own receipt: at the end of Guardians, the request that proposed 1.6.0 and every guardian vote on it, each a tap away on Solscan.

Old notifications from 1.x are now cleared as soon as the update lands, even before the app is opened.
```

## Testing instructions for the reviewer (additions for 1.6.0)

4. **Guardians** needs no wallet. It reads one JSON from our server
   (`https://skr.alexkosa.dev/api/store-guardians`), which reads the store's
   guardian program `GUARDsVUfAaydbPU6QGcpWRLUkExaULtaygwEtkdhZAD` on chain.
   This build's own receipt appears at the end of the tab once its guardian
   votes are on chain. App names and icons come from the store's public
   catalog; for an app the store does not list yet, the name is the one its
   publisher registered on chain and the icon is read from the launcher icon
   of its release APK (the store's own storage or the publisher's public R2
   bucket, by range requests). Nothing is signed.
5. Notifications: the only notifications are the position check-ins (Me,
   Off/1/2/3 a day). `USE_EXACT_ALARM` is requested so a check-in arrives at
   the hour the person chose; it is used for nothing else. A small receiver
   on `MY_PACKAGE_REPLACED` / `BOOT_COMPLETED` deletes notifications scheduled
   by older versions, so they stop without the app being opened.
