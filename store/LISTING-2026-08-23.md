## Long description

Watch the SKR staking vault and your own position in it, and stake without
leaving the app.

Your card shows what you hold, how long you have held it — read from the oldest
signature on your position account, not guessed — your weight in the vault, and
the exact countdown to your unlock.

Pulse shows the whole vault: active stake, share of supply, the flow of the last
24 hours, 7 days or 30 days, and how the exit queue matures over the next 48
hours. Flow is the live floor: every finalized stake, unstake and withdrawal as
it lands, with the day's largest move pinned above the stream. Queue shows what
is waiting to leave and when it frees. Alerts wake you on this phone when your
own cooldown ends.

Staking happens through your wallet. The app prepares the transactions, your
wallet approves and broadcasts them, and the app follows every signature until
the chain confirms it. One approval can carry up to sixteen separate stakes.

Every number carries its source: commitment, slot, freshness, and the account it
came from. Values are labelled exact, estimated or unavailable, and incomplete
history is never presented as complete.

SKR Eyes never asks for a seed phrase, never holds keys, and never takes custody
of funds. Your private key stays in Seed Vault on your phone.

SKR Eyes is an independent product. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or
sponsored by Solana Foundation or Solana Mobile.

## Testing instructions for the reviewer

The whole app except staking works without holding any SKR:

1. Open **Me** and paste any public address that holds a SKR position, for
   example `BmJaEmaEURBDpj91GUTvJcpKy2MAu8ZtRFRPaMpEuuax` — the developer's own
   wallet, offered so no third party's position is used as a fixture. The card
   fills with that address's position, age and vault weight. No signature is
   requested and no wallet connection is needed.
2. **Pulse**, **Flow** and **Queue** need no wallet at all.
3. **Alerts** asks for the notification permission and can send a test
   notification immediately.
4. To test staking, connect a wallet holding at least 1 SKR, enter an amount per
   transaction, choose how many transactions, and approve once in the wallet.
   The protocol minimum is 1 SKR per transaction.
5. **The example address has no unstake running.** Its cooldown countdown is
   therefore not displayed — there is nothing pending to count down to. The
   maturing of the exit queue can be seen on **Queue**, which needs no wallet.
6. **On `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`:** an unlock is a single on-chain timestamp, and
   the whole promise of the Alerts screen is that the phone wakes at that
   moment rather than "some time that hour". The alert is scheduled locally,
   there is no push server, and the permission stays revocable by the user.
   `USE_EXACT_ALARM` is deliberately not requested.
