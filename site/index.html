Four tabs, one idea

Every number on every screen comes from the chain and carries its source. Nothing is decorative, nothing is invented to fill a gap. Every phone in the vault is a finalized move you can tap for its receipt.

Vault

The home screen. Stakes fall into the pile of what stays, larger ones larger and slower, a 100K stake as a shower. Exits hang above it with a ring that closes over their 48 hours. Under the scene: how much is staked, the day drawn hour by hour, and the exit queue. Motion can be Live, Calm or Off.

Flow

The live floor. Every finalized stake, exit and withdrawal as it lands, filtered by kind and by size, with the day's biggest moves pinned on top. A large stake rings an old phone, a large exit lands with a low boom, on any screen, each with a short buzz. Or switch both off.

Me

Your position, what it has earned, read from the chain, and staking without leaving the app: one approval covers up to sixteen parts, or tap Sixteen for 16 parts of 1 SKR as a small daily habit. The app checks your balance first and says plainly what you can afford.

Your card

Your .skr name from the registry, your days in stake read from the oldest signature on your position account, your weight in the vault. Share it as a picture, with your name or your amount hidden if you prefer.

Alerts

An alarm at the exact on-chain unlock time, set on the phone itself. Sound and buzz switches, the language of the app, and a tip in SKR to kosa.skr that is staked right away, all of it.

How it works

The short version: your keys stay in your phone, our server never touches them, and every claim on screen can be checked on chain.

your keys
The app cannot read your seed phrase or private key. Seed Vault holds them, your wallet approves every transaction, and the wallet — not this app — broadcasts it.
your data
No account, no sign-up, no analytics. The public address you connect is stored on the phone so you are not asked twice, and Disconnect erases it.
the numbers
An independent indexer reads the staking program directly and labels every value exact, estimated or unavailable, and every reading fresh, aging or stale.
the history
Coverage begins when the indexer began. Where history is incomplete the app says so, instead of showing a partial picture as a whole one.
alerts
Scheduled by the phone itself, using the exact on-chain unlock time. No push server, nothing about you leaves the device.

Questions

do I need SKR?
No. Vault, Flow and Alerts work with no wallet at all, and you can look up any public address on Me. SKR is needed only to stake.
what does staking cost?
The network fee, a few thousand lamports per transaction. A wallet's first stake also pays rent for its position account, which stays with the account. The app takes nothing.
why several transactions?
Because each one is a separate on-chain stake. The app can send up to sixteen from a single approval, and each needs at least 1 SKR.
which languages?
English, Deutsch, 中文, Русский, Español, Português, Türkçe, Tiếng Việt, Indonesia, 한국어, 日本語. The app follows the phone's language and can be switched by hand on Alerts.
who made it?
Alex Kosa, an SKR staker who wanted this for himself. Source under MIT at github.com/busminer/skr-ecosystem-eyes. Questions: skr-eyes@alexkosa.dev.
