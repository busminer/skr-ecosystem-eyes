Privacy Policy

SKR Eyes has no accounts, no sign-up, no advertising and no analytics. This page explains exactly what the app keeps on your phone, what our server keeps, what is written to the Solana blockchain for good, and how to erase what can be erased.

Last updated 1 October 2026

Who is responsible

SKR Eyes is an independent product built and operated by Alex Kosa. Questions about this policy or about your data go to skr-eyes@alexkosa.dev.

What SKR Eyes never collects

  • No name, email, phone number or any other identity information.
  • No account: there is nothing to register and nothing to log in to.
  • No seed phrase and no private key. The app cannot read them; they stay inside Seed Vault on your phone.
  • No fingerprint. The fingerprint sensor only tells Android that your finger was there; the app and our server never receive a fingerprint or any image of it.
  • No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no tracking pixels, no third-party SDKs that profile you.
  • No contacts, photos, location or files.

What is stored on your phone

All of this lives only on the device:

  • The public address of the wallet you connected, the name the wallet reported for it, and the authorization token your wallet issued, so the app can reconnect quietly instead of asking for approval on every launch. Disconnect erases the token.
  • Your .skr name, if your wallet has one, so the app can greet you by it and your reminders can say it.
  • Your alert settings and the Blink reminders scheduled with the Android notification system. The reminders are scheduled on the phone itself; nothing about them leaves it.
  • If you verify your Seeker for an Eye: a signing key created inside the phone's StrongBox secure element. It cannot leave the chip and only signs after your fingerprint.

What our server keeps

When you use the Eye, our server at skr.alexkosa.dev keeps:

  • Your wallet's public address and your Eye: its number, its asset address, its streak, its Blinks and the transactions that paid for them (SKR payments and the network fees around them).
  • For the fingerprint check: the public half of the StrongBox key and the certificate chain Google's hardware attestation produced for it. That chain describes the device (brand and model, Android version and security patch level, whether the boot is verified and the bootloader locked) and this app; it contains no fingerprint and no personal name.
  • The Eyes you looked at ("looks") and the app version that asked.
  • Operational logs, which include wallet addresses, for at most 30 days.

To open an Eye the server checks, from public Solana data, that the wallet holds a Seeker Genesis Token and an active SKR stake. To show your staking position the app sends the public wallet address you chose; the server reads public Solana data for it and answers.

The world of Eyes

Every Eye that has been opened appears in the world of Eyes inside the app, drawn as a small eye with its number, its streak, whether it blinked today and, if its wallet has a .skr name, that name. These are facts that are already public on Solana; the app adds nothing to them. Other people can look at your Eye, and you can look at theirs, up to five looks a day. A look tells the owner that somebody looked, and the server keeps who looked at whom for the counter and for the list of people who looked at you. Nobody can look at anything else about you.

What is on the blockchain for good

Your Eye is an NFT (a Metaplex Core asset) in your wallet on Solana. Its number, streak, Blinks and picture address are public on the blockchain and cannot be erased by us or by anyone. The picture shows your Eye's number and streak and may show other public facts about your wallet. Every payment is a public Solana transaction.

How to delete what can be deleted

  • On the phone: open Me and tap Disconnect. That erases the stored address, the wallet name and the wallet authorization token. Uninstalling the app removes everything else, including scheduled notifications.
  • On the server: write to skr-eyes@alexkosa.dev with a message signed by the wallet; we delete the fingerprint binding and the looks for that wallet. The ledger of Eyes and payments mirrors the public blockchain and is kept, because the blockchain keeps it anyway.

Server logs and Cloudflare

Our server and the Cloudflare network in front of it process the IP address of each request to deliver the answer and to limit abuse. We do not sell it and do not build profiles from it. Requests are counted for rate limiting in memory only.

Third parties

  • Cloudflare delivers this site and the app's API.
  • A Solana RPC provider is used by our server to read the blockchain and send transactions. The app never contacts it directly and never holds its credentials.
  • Google's hardware attestation roots are used by our server to check the StrongBox key.
  • Your wallet application (Seed Vault Wallet) shows and signs every transaction under its own privacy policy.

Children

SKR Eyes is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 13.
