What SKR Eyes is

SKR Eyes is an independent application for SKR stakers on Solana Mobile Seeker. It shows staking data read from the blockchain, and it lets the holder of a Seeker open one Eye per wallet and blink it once every 8 hours. The app itself does not stake, unstake or withdraw anything.

The Eye

  • An Eye can be opened by a wallet that holds a Seeker Genesis Token and has an active SKR stake. The server checks both from public blockchain data, and the phone proves once, with a fingerprint, that it is a Seeker.
  • The Eye is a collectible NFT (Metaplex Core) minted to your wallet. It is frozen: it cannot be sold or transferred while it is frozen, and only the collection owner's wallet can thaw it.
  • Opening an Eye and each Blink are paid in SKR, from your wallet to the maker of SKR Eyes. The amounts are set by the service and shown in SKR in the app before your wallet asks you to approve; the first 1,000 Eyes open for one amount and later Eyes for another, and the amounts can change for Eyes not yet opened. Opening an Eye also puts a storage deposit, in SOL, into the Eye's own account on Solana, where it stays, and pays Metaplex's create fee (0.0015 SOL today, held in the same account and collectable by Metaplex). Every transaction pays a network fee in SOL set by your wallet, so you need some SOL in the wallet as well as SKR. Payments are final and are not refunded.
  • The streak counts days in a row with at least one Blink (UTC days). A missed day starts it over. We may restore a day lost because of a failure on our side.
  • The Eye gives no rights, income or share of anything. No token, airdrop or reward is promised for holding an Eye or for a streak.

No custody

SKR Eyes never holds or moves your funds. Every transaction is shown by your wallet and signed by you. The app checks every transaction before asking you to sign it: you pay the network fee yourself, SKR goes only to the maker's SKR account written in the app, no SOL is paid to the maker, and no other token is touched. Accuracy and its limits

Blockchain data can be delayed, reorganised or temporarily unreachable. Where the app cannot be sure of a number it says so instead of presenting a guess as exact.
