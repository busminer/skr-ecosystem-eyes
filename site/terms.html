What SKR Eyes is

SKR Eyes is an independent analytics application for SKR staking on Solana. It reads publicly available on-chain data, presents your own staking position, and prepares stake transactions that your wallet approves and broadcasts.

No custody

SKR Eyes never holds, moves or has access to your funds. It cannot read your seed phrase or private key. Every transaction is approved by you inside your wallet application, and the wallet — not this app — broadcasts it.

Your responsibility

You decide what to stake and how much. Staking SKR starts a cooldown period defined by the on-chain program, currently 48 hours, before an unstaked amount can be withdrawn. Transactions on a blockchain are final and cannot be reversed by us or by anyone else.

Not financial advice

Nothing in the app or on this site is investment, financial, legal or tax advice. Numbers describe what happened on chain; they do not predict anything and do not recommend anything.

Accuracy and its limits

The app is built so that every number carries its source and its state: values are labelled exact, estimated or unavailable, and data is labelled fresh, aging or stale. Historical coverage begins when our indexer began; the app says so rather than presenting a partial history as complete. Blockchain data can still be delayed, reorganised or temporarily unreachable.
