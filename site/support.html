Support

One person builds this app. Write to him directly — there is no ticket queue and no bot in between.

email
skr-eyes@alexkosa.dev — written as plain text so it works without scripts: skr-eyes at alexkosa dot dev. Expect an answer within a couple of days.
bugs and requests
Open an issue at github.com/busminer/skr-ecosystem-eyes. Public issues get fixed faster, because anyone hitting the same thing can add to them.
what to include
The screen you were on, what you expected, what happened, and the app version from the store listing. If it concerns a transaction, the signature — it is public, and with it the chain can be asked directly. Never send a seed phrase or a private key to anyone, including us. We will never ask.
a transaction went wrong
The app builds transactions; your wallet signs and broadcasts them, and the chain decides. That means nobody here can reverse, cancel or refund one — not us, not the wallet. What we can do is read the signature with you and say exactly what the chain recorded.
your unlock is late
Cooldown is enforced by the staking program, not by this app. The countdown you see is read from your position account. If it disagrees with what you expect, send the signature and we will look at the same data together.
